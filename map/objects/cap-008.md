# cap-008 — Citation-first answer engine routing

Every answer produced by a research workflow ends with a citation block: source title, URL, provider that returned it, and a snippet of the relevant content. The block is rendered into the WhatsApp digest (per the ICM weekly digest shape) and into the NotebookLM-imported sources list. No answer ships without citations.

## Why this shape

The seed brainstorm and the 2026-08-30 distillation both flagged "citation drift" as a recurring failure mode: agents return plausible-looking answers without surfacing where the fact came from. A citation-first default makes "no source" a build break, not a stylistic miss. This is also the shape Dan already accepts in the ICM weekly digest (`*Listen on your phone:*` + `*One-page study guide:*` + `*Open notebook:*` blocks).

## Shape

- **Citation block schema**: `{provider, query, url, title, snippet, retrieved_at}`
- **Rendering**: per-delivery-channel templates (WhatsApp plain text, Google Docs rich text, NotebookLM source list)
- **Validation gate**: any answer without at least 1 citation per non-trivial claim fails the cron

## Connected to

- **owns:** the citation-block schema + per-channel templates
- **owned-by:** the synthesis output layer (any synthesis that ships goes through citation-first rendering)
- **joins:** `cap-009` (Markdown report generation — uses the same schema for embedded citations); `cap-012` (Automated claims verification — sits between synthesis and citation, verifying claims *before* they're cited)
- **looks-like-but-is-not:** post-hoc citation patching (taking an answer and appending a source URL at the end). cap-008 mandates *first-class* citations at the schema level.

## If you change this

- **Hits:** every delivery channel (WhatsApp, Docs, NotebookLM); the cron output schemas; the digest templates in `/opt/data/digests/_templates/record-template.md`; the citation-block validators in any cron job
- **Does not hit:** pure-format outputs (e.g. `robots.txt`-style text without claims); raw extracted content before synthesis
